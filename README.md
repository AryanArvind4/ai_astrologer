## 🌌 About the Project

**AI Astrologer** is a vibe-coded web app that gives users a mystical, interactive astrology experience. Enter your name, birth details, and favorite number — and let the stars (and GPT) reveal your destiny.

✨ This isn’t just another chatbot — it’s:
- An **AI-powered astrologer**
- A **real-time personality reader**
- A **planetary position calculator**
- A **life-chart visualizer**

---

## ⚙️ Features

▸ **User Input**: Name, Date of Birth, Time, Place, and Lucky Number  
▸ **GPT-Powered Reading**: Astrology + numerology analysis  
▸ **Zodiac Visualization**: Beautiful card layout of planetary positions  
▸ **Radar Chart**: See your Love, Career, Health, and Spirituality as a visual graph  
▸ **Chatbot Mode**: Ask anything — “Will I get a job soon?” 👀  
▸ **Streamlit UI**: Dark theme with celestial gradient ✨
